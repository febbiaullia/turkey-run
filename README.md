Gobble & Go — The Great Turkey Escape: a side-scrolling runner where you play a turkey fleeing Thanksgiving dinner. Outrun the farmer through the farmyard, the barnyard and the dreaded kitchen; jump fences and hay bales, duck under flying carving knives, dodge dogs and flaming ovens, and grab corn to stay ahead. Three hearts, a farmer who gains on every stumble, golden corn bursts, stage banners, synth gobbles and a best score to chase.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
